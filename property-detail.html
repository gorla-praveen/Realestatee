About This Property

Welcome to this stunning 4BHK luxury villa nestled in the prestigious Road No. 12, Banjara Hills — one of Hyderabad's most coveted residential addresses. Spanning 3,200 sq ft of thoughtfully designed living space across three floors, this villa offers an exceptional blend of modern architecture and premium finishes.

The property features a private swimming pool, landscaped garden, and a spacious terrace with breathtaking views of Banjara Hills. The interiors are designed by a renowned Hyderabad-based interior designer, with imported Italian marble flooring, modular kitchen with Bosch appliances, and floor-to-ceiling windows that flood every room with natural light.

Located minutes from Jubilee Hills, Film Nagar, and major IT corridors, this property offers the perfect balance of luxury living and urban convenience. Excellent connectivity to the Outer Ring Road (ORR), MMTS stations, and top schools such as Oakridge International and DPS.
